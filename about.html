How MAIS works

How problems are listed, how solutions are checked, and how to take part.

The life cycle

Open problems are incorporated into agendas, and a maturing agenda spins off papers. Identifiers never change.

MAIS-OnOpen problem. A short exposition of one problem.
MAIS-AnAgenda. A cluster of related problems with partial progress.
MAIS-PnPaper. A paper in progress that grows out of solutions.

Origin

MAIS grew out of Lionel Levine’s survey Math for AI Safety: An Invitation for Mathematicians.

How to take part

  1. Open an issue naming the problem, before investing serious work.
  2. Post partial or numerical results, labeled as such.
  3. A maintainer reviews it in public and records what was checked and what was not.
  4. Verified work is credited as printed on your write-up.

AI assistance

AI coauthors are welcome. If an AI produced the argument, say so: such submissions are labeled and screened first, and every claim is still verified by a person. The review does not replace the judgment of the researcher who submits the work.
