About

How MAIS works

A registry that keeps problems precise, checks solutions in public, and credits contributors as they wish to be credited.

The life cycle

An open problem is incorporated into an agenda, and a maturing agenda spins off papers. Identifiers never change.

MAIS-OnOpen problem. A short exposition of a single problem.
MAIS-AnAgenda. A cluster of related problems and partial progress.
MAIS-PnPaper. Work in progress that grows out of solutions.

How to take part

  1. Open an issue naming the problem, before investing serious work.
  2. Post partial or numerical results, labeled as such.
  3. A maintainer reviews in public and records what was and was not checked.
  4. Verified work is credited as printed on your write-up.

On AI assistance

AI coauthors are welcome. Submissions whose authors say an AI produced the argument are labeled and screened first, and every claim still has to be verified by a person. The review process does not replace the judgment of the researcher who submits the work.
