Agendas

Eight research directions

An agenda is a cluster of related open problems together with partial progress on them.

Criteria

What a problem needs

  1. A statement precise enough for a mathematician outside the area to work on.
  2. A theory of change: how a solution would advance AI safety.
  3. A literature check. “Open” means open to our knowledge.
  4. A permanent identifier that never changes.
  5. Public verification of any claimed solution.
